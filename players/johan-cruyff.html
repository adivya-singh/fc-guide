Player Overview

Cruyff brings total football to your attack. Elite dribbling, vision, and composure make him a meta pick for players who love technical, possession-based play up front.

Key Strengths

  • World-class dribbling
  • 5-star skill moves
  • Excellent agility
  • Great link-up play
