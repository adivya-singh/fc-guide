Winger

Gareth Bale

The Welsh Wizard

Rating: 111 Position: RW / ST Nation: Wales Card: Icon Striker Card

Player Overview

Bale combines raw pace with a thunderous left foot. Ideal for counter-attacking football and cutting inside for long-range screamers.

Key Strengths

  • Blistering pace
  • Powerful long shots
  • Great in the air
  • Strong sprint dribbles

Watch Out For

Ball control at top speed needs practice.
