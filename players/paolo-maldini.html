Centreback

Paolo Maldini

Il Capitano

Rating: 111 Position: CB / LB Nation: Italy Card: Icon Defender Card

Player Overview

Maldini is the gold standard for defending. Interceptions, standing tackles, and positioning make him near-unbeatable at the back.

Key Strengths

  • Elite defending stats
  • Great pace for a CB
  • Can play fullback
  • Excellent positioning

Watch Out For

Heading against tall strikers needs support.
