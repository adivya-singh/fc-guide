Winger

Lionel Messi

La Pulga

Rating: 111 Position: RW / CAM Nation: Argentina Card: Inter Miami

Player Overview

Messi remains a meta winger with unmatched dribbling and finesse shots. His low centre of gravity makes him nearly impossible to dispossess in tight spaces.

Key Strengths

  • Best-in-class dribbling
  • Finesse shot specialist
  • Excellent free kicks
  • Tight turning radius

Watch Out For

Physical battles against tall centre-backs can be tough.
